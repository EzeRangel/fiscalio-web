# Product Definition: Fiscalio

## Vision
Fiscalio is a web application designed to help Mexican freelancers and independent contractors under the RESICO (Régimen Simplificado de Confianza) regime calculate and manage their taxes effortlessly. It bridges the gap between complex tax mechanics and everyday freelance operations, answering the question: "How much of my money is actually mine?"

## Target Audience
- Mexican freelancers (software developers, designers, writers, consultants).
- Independent contractors billing domestic (Persona Moral/Física) or foreign clients.
- Professionals seeking a simple, automated way to estimate ISR, IVA, and retentions without spreadsheet complexity.

## Core Features
1. **RESICO Tax Calculator:**
   - Real-time ISR and IVA computation.
   - Support for both Gross-to-Net and Net-to-Gross (gross-up) calculations.
   - Automatic adjustment for client type (Persona Moral vs. Persona Física) and residency (National 16% IVA vs. Export/Foreign 0% IVA).
2. **Educational Content System:**
   - In-depth, hand-crafted Spanish blog posts explaining tax procedures, dates, and common pitfalls.
   - Structured resources to build trust and drive organic SEO traffic.
3. **Waitlist & Lead Acquisition:**
   - Integrated signup widget writing leads to Airtable.
   - Automation of confirmation and transactional emails via Resend.
4. **Admin Dashboard & Prototyping Tools:**
   - Graphics Designer tool to preview, dimension, and export custom SVG/PNG diagrams.
   - Carousel generators for LinkedIn marketing.
   - Email template visualizers and design previews.
   - PDF templates for client reports and tax checklists.

## Product Principles
- **Simplicity first:** Avoid accounting jargon. Use plain language to explain concepts.
- **Client-Side Privacy:** Perform calculations client-side where possible to maintain speed and data privacy.
- **Brand Consistency:** Respect clean typography (DM Sans and Geist Mono) and premium, minimal aesthetics.
