# Content Voice

## Domain & Language

- Product is Spanish-first (`<html lang="es">`, `locale: "es_MX"`). Keep UI copy in Spanish unless explicitly asked.
- Domain: Mexico **RESICO** tax (ISR + IVA) for freelancers. Rates in `lib/tax-constants.ts` must match SAT rules — change them only when the official table changes, and verify with the existing test cases before shipping.

## Writing and Structure Guidelines

Non-Commoditized Approach: Don't simply recycle basic information that anyone can find on the SAT website (that's commoditized content). Instead, offer a unique perspective, such as "mental models" that provide clarity for the reader.

Experience-Based Title: Create a compelling title focused on the user's pain point or transformation. Avoid generic titles (e.g., "RESICO Guide 2026") and opt for those that promise relief or debunk a myth.

Empathetic Introduction: Begin by validating the freelancer's tax anxiety regarding the SAT. Present the core mental model from the start to capture their attention.

Clear and Human Structure: Organize the text with a stressed human reader in mind. Use digestible paragraphs and conversational headings (H2, H3) that guide the reader logically.

No AI Hacks: Write naturally. Don't use artificial tactics like excessive text chunking or sacrificing fluency to force exact keywords; current search systems understand synonyms and general meaning perfectly.

Visual Support Markers: Google's AI values ​​high-quality visual resources. Include clear instructions in brackets (e.g., [INSERT INFOGRAPHIC ABOUT X HERE]) in sections where an image or diagram would simplify a complex explanation.

Satisfaction Closing (Golden Rule): The article should end by answering this question: "Does the reader feel satisfied, relieved, and equipped with useful tools after reading this?" Conclude by positioning Fiscalio as the natural and stress-free solution for offline accounting, without overwhelming the reader with aggressive sales tactics.
